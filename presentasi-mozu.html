🗄️ Database Schema

📋 users

id, name, email, password, role

Data customer & admin

📦 products

id, name, description, price, stock, image

Katalog menu Jasuke

🛒 orders

order_number, user_id, customer_name, total_amount, status

Master pesanan

📝 order_items

order_id, product_id, quantity, price, subtotal

Detail item pesanan

💳 payments

order_id, payment_method, amount, status

Transaksi pembayaran

⚙️ settings

id, qris_image

Konfigurasi QRIS

🔗 Relasi Database

users → orders (1:N)
orders → order_items (1:N)
products → order_items (1:N)
orders → payments (1:1)
